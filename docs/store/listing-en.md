# DentHub-Brush – store listing (English)

**Short description (80 chars):** A brushing timer that hears you brush – and connects you with your dentist.

**Description:**
DentHub-Brush times your brushing by sound, automatically – with manual, sonic and oscillating brushes, on
your phone and smartwatch. No buttons: it listens from turning off the tap to rinsing, and tells you when a
few seconds are still missing.

- Automatic timing by sound (no recording, everything stays on your phone)
- Quadrant cues, goals, achievements, kids' mode with stickers
- Journal: floss, mouthwash, bleeding gums, sensitivity
- Record a complaint step by step with a tooth map and photos – advice, and send it to your doctor if needed
- Appointment requests, check-up reminders, medicine and care reminders
- Electric brush battery watch
- Link with your clinic on DentHub
- Encrypted backup, family transfer, PDF report for your dentist

The advice is general information and does not replace a dental examination.
